# Brand Story

Build a complete brand identity in one session: name → tagline → about text → voice guide.
Output is platform-ready — paste directly into Etsy, Amazon, Shopify, or any independent store.

## Input

User provides:
- **Product category** — what they sell (e.g. "handmade crochet tops", "soy candles", "macrame wall art")
- **Target buyer** — who buys it (e.g. "women 25–40 who love slow fashion", "gift buyers", "home decor enthusiasts")
- **Vibe / mood** — 2–3 words or a reference (e.g. "minimal and earthy", "cozy and nostalgic", "bold and playful")
- **Origin story** (optional) — why they started, what makes it personal

If any are missing, ask before proceeding.

## Steps

### 1. Draft brand foundations

From the inputs, generate:

**Name candidates (3 options)**
- Each should be: short (1–2 words), memorable, available as a domain concept, evocative of the vibe
- Mix approaches: one abstract (feeling), one descriptive (material/craft), one founder-personal

**Tagline (2 options per name)**
- Under 8 words
- Leads with emotion or benefit, not process
- Avoid: "handmade with love", "quality you can trust", "made just for you"

### 2. Write the About / Bio text

Write two versions:

**Short bio (50–80 words)**
— For Etsy shop bio, Amazon brand story module, Instagram profile
— Opens with what the buyer gets, not the seller's backstory
— Ends with a subtle call to feeling (not "buy now")

**Long bio (150–200 words)**
— For Shopify About page, independent store, brand deck
— Structure: hook → origin story → craft/process → what it means for the buyer
— Conversational, first person, no corporate language

### 3. Brand voice guide (one paragraph)

Define how this brand writes in 4 dimensions:
- **Tone** — e.g. warm and direct / playful but not silly / quiet confidence
- **Words it uses** — 5 example words/phrases that fit
- **Words it avoids** — 3 things that feel off-brand
- **One example sentence** — rewrites a generic line in this brand's voice

## Output

```
## 🦜 Brand Story — [Product Category]

### name options
1. [Name] — [one-line rationale]
   Tagline A: "..."
   Tagline B: "..."
2. [Name] — [one-line rationale]
   ...
3. [Name] — [one-line rationale]
   ...

### short bio
[50–80 words, platform-ready]

### long bio
[150–200 words, platform-ready]

### brand voice
Tone: [description]
Use: [word], [word], [word], [word], [word]
Avoid: [word], [word], [word]
Sounds like: "[example sentence rewritten in brand voice]"
```

Report is complete when all four sections are filled and the short bio could be pasted
into an Etsy about section without editing.
