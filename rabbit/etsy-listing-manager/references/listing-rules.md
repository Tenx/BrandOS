# Listing Rules And QA

## Etsy Limits

- Title maximum: 140 characters.
- Maximum tags per listing: 13.
- Maximum tag length: 20 characters.

## Core QA

- Title present, natural, and within limit.
- Description says exactly what is sold in the first sentence.
- Tags are present, relevant, non-duplicative, and within limits.
- Materials and attributes match the product.
- Price is in the expected currency and range.
- Quantity and variations are present when needed.
- Processing time and made-to-order status are clear.
- Images are present, product-focused, and not accidentally duplicated.

## Measurement Sanity

- Crop tops: length often 35-45 cm.
- Regular tops: length often 55-70 cm.
- Long sweaters: length often 65-80 cm.
- Maxi dresses: length often 130-150 cm.
- Tops/sweaters bust range is often 80-122 cm, wider for oversized fits.

These are sanity ranges, not facts. Ask for real measurements before publishing when uncertain.

## Category Keywords

- `crystal_bracelets`: bracelet, crystal, amethyst, rose quartz, citrine, quartz, gemstone, beaded bracelet
- `sweaters_cardigans`: sweater, cardigan, turtleneck, jumper, pullover, hooded cardigan
- `crochet_clothing`: crochet, halter top, tank top, dress, mesh top, fishnet, crop top
- `jewelry`: necklace, ring, earrings, pendant, jewelry, jewellery
- `home_decor`: candle, print, poster, vase, wall art, decor

## Compliance

For crystals and spiritual jewelry, avoid claims that sound medical, guaranteed, or disease-related:

- cure
- heal disease
- anxiety cure
- depression cure
- guaranteed luck
- 100% effective

Safer framing: material, bead size, color, craftsmanship, gift occasion, symbolic meaning, mindfulness or calming aesthetic without guaranteed outcomes.

## Current Market Checks

If the user asks for competitor pricing, current SEO trends, or market comparisons, browse current sources before making claims.
