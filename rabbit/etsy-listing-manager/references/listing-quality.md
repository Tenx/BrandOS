# Listing Quality

## Required Sections

- `## TITLE`
- `## DESCRIPTION`
- `## TAGS`
- `## MATERIAL TAGS`
- `## ATTRIBUTES`

## Title

- Lead with what the item is.
- Front-load color, material, product type, style, and handmade/vintage trait when true.
- Keep it natural and readable.
- Stay under Etsy's 140-character title limit.

## Description

- First sentence must clearly say exactly what is being sold.
- Include style, construction, fit, color, material, key details, size/care notes, customization status, and processing time when known.
- Keep buyer-facing language warm, specific, and concrete.
- Do not invent measurements, materials, certifications, policy terms, or shipping guarantees.

## Tags

- Use exactly 13 Etsy tags when possible.
- Each tag must be 20 characters or fewer.
- Tags should match buyer search phrases: material, product type, color, style, occasion, recipient, handmade, made to order.
- Avoid duplicate ideas unless they reflect different search behavior.

## Uncertainty

If material, sizing, care, or customization is unknown, ask or use cautious language. Do not overclaim.
